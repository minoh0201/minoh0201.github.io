Quality

Our product standards

Dietary supplements are only as good as their verification. These requirements apply to every Minova product and every manufacturing partner.

  • cGMP manufacturing (21 CFR Part 111)FDA-registered facilities with third-party GMP registration such as NSF.
  • Certificate of Analysis for every lotIdentity, potency, heavy metals and microbial limits verified by an ISO/IEC 17025 laboratory.
  • Guaranteed active contentLabel claims stated per serving and confirmed by testing, not by raw-material assumptions.
  • Marketplace complianceLabels follow FDA/DSHEA rules; products complete Amazon's dietary supplement verification before listing.
